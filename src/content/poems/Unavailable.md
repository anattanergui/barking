# Unavailable
Sometimes
Perhaps often times
It takes a long time 

Perhaps most of one’s life
In most cases, if not all

To see, and realise
That any face
Any sound
Anything

Not available 
To senses
At any moment

Does not exist
Is lost
Gone
Dead

If, the next day
It returns
That is
Reincarnation

Of a memory
In the hope of which
With belief in which
Most people
Most of the time
Live their lives

So often 
Looking past
The incarnated

Unavailable 
To beauty
Reverence
