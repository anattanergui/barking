# About The Individual
Individual is sovereign 
On the grounds that
His inner experience is sealed
Unavailable to any other
Whatever may be done
To the individual

And on the grounds — though it can be made
Deniable to him —
That he can end his life

What does this sovereignty 
Give the individual 
What does sovereignty consist of
What are its constituents 

Form — the individual — and
Awareness 
Without either — there’s no individual 
No sealing of inner experience
No question of self-destruction

Does the sovereignty then lie
In the individual — form
Or awareness 
Which of these begets the other
Bestows upon the other 
Sovereignty

There can be only two possibilities
Awareness bestows its sovereignty
Its indestructibility 
Upon form

Or 

There is no bestowing
Awareness without form 
Form without awareness 
Is incomprehensible 

If the first — then individual — form
Gets nothing out of his sovereignty 
For individual is dust 

And — in that case — awareness too 
Gets nothing out of its sovereignty 
Out of bestowing it upon form
If it has to keep creating forms, to ensure 
That its sovereignty sustains
And without form — its sovereignty 
Goes unnoticed — 
To itself it may presume sovereignty 
But without witness — without form — 
The sovereignty of awareness is empty 

Even in bestowing its indestructible 
Sovereignty upon form, in form
Awareness is helpless — because without bestowal
Its sovereignty remains unheld
Even by itself

And if —
There is no bestowing
As awareness without form and form 
Without awareness is incomprehensible 
Then sovereignty 
For form or awareness 
Remains worthless

No side can benefit from it
No side can lay a claim on it
No side owns it, or can exercise it

Individual sovereignty then
Based on the ground that his experience 
Is sealed

Is worthless to form — individual
And to awareness, consciousness — sealed within
Or floating without form

Recognition of awareness — as a principle 
Independent of form 
Or as 
Constituent of form — inseparable from form
Is worthless 
To form — for it is dust
And to awareness, for it is either already form 
Or awareness requires no recognition from form

The worth of worth is worthless
Of recognition, of recognition of sovereignty 
Of its absence, of unity or of separation
From the point of view of either —
Form or awareness 
Or both, as inseparable 
The worth of any worth 
Is worthless — is worthlessness 

And all that remains
Is this — this happening 
This coincidence of form and awareness
Happening
This expression, comprehension, 
Awareness and form cohabiting 
Happening 

Is all that remains
And it cannot not remain
The conception, imagination 
Of this happening — not happening 
Is impossible

Whatever it is named — this happening — 
It’s not worth anything 
For form, for awareness, for either or for both
There’s no benefit 
No reward, no purpose, no motive
No meaning — for form 
Or for awareness, or for both at once

Its not-happening is the one impossibility 

And simultaneously — inexorably —
For consciousness to not be 
For individual consciousness 
To not be — is impossible

Sovereignty undeniable 
And worthless
