# Elegy To Life
Dead people
Strangers crossing streets
Family sharing paths, glanced 
In corridors, rooms — people
In houses no different 
From offices, tea room
Some morgue
Some walk

Dead walking, sitting
Familiar, unfamiliar 
Each word, already spoken
Heard — yesterday
Same gesture, same week
Same event
Next year
A repeat

Seeing — not
Looking
Not touching — sensing
Not listning
Words uttered, hollowed
Of emotion
Steps taken
Emptied of passion

In dreams
Of nights, in thoughts
Of days
Under the flickering
Of a sun ray
Around death
In grief

Dead eyes
Light up
Through tear drop
A howl

Give way
