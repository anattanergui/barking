# Meaning
It is not speaking
Not talking
Not writing
But communicating

That is
The greatest challenge
The impossible
Meaning, demand — ideal — 
That language
Proposes

The only possible way
To undercut it
Is seeing
That communicating
Just as speaking

Talking, and writing
Happens not
To
Another

But only
Always
With
Oneself
